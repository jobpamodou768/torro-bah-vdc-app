PROJECT INFORMATION

About this prototype

This is the first phone-friendly prototype for the VDC Management System. It demonstrates officer-specific digital templates and printable records.

Included workspaces

  • Chairperson and Vice Chairperson
  • Secretary General and Assistant Secretary
  • Treasurer and Auditor
  • Project Officer and Procurement Officer
  • Mobilisation Officer and Welfare Officer
  • PRO and Assistant PRO

Backup and portability

Export a JSON backup to keep a copy of records. This is not encrypted storage.

Not production-ready for confidential use. This prototype has no individual login, server backup, multi-user synchronisation or role-based security.

Governance safeguard

The app is an administrative tool. It does not itself approve spending, change committee decisions or replace community consultation and legally required permissions.
