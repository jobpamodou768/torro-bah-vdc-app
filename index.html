PROJECT INFORMATION

About this prototype

This is the first phone-friendly prototype for the Torro Bah VDC Management System. It demonstrates officer-specific digital templates and printable records.

Included workspaces

  • Chairperson and Vice Chairperson
  • Secretary General and Assistant Secretary
  • Treasurer and Auditor
  • Project Officer and Procurement Officer
  • Mobilisation Officer and Welfare Officer
  • PRO and Assistant PRO

Backup and portability

Export a JSON backup to keep a copy of records. Import it on this device or another compatible copy of the app. This is not encrypted storage.

Not production-ready for confidential use. This prototype has no individual login, server backup, multi-user synchronisation, role-based security or legally validated approval workflow. Those are required before operational deployment.

Governance safeguard

The app is an administrative tool. It does not itself approve spending, change committee decisions, authorise land transactions or replace community consultation and legally required permissions.
